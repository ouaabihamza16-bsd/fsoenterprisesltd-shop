About the company

A small property company that works only with what it owns

FSO Enterprises Ltd is a privately owned property company. It was incorporated on 11 November 2019 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 12307091, with its registered office in Erith, England.

The company buys property for its own portfolio, lets that property directly, looks after it while it is let, and from time to time sells property it owns. Its registered activities are the buying and selling of its own real estate and the letting and operating of its own or leased real estate.

It is owner-managed: the company's director is also its majority shareholder. Anyone renting from the company is dealing with the owner of the property, with no agent in between.

Not an agent

FSO Enterprises Ltd is not a letting agent, estate agent or managing agent. Everything it does relates to its own property.

  • It does not let or manage property for other landlords.
  • It does not market or sell property on behalf of other owners.
  • It does not offer property services to third parties.

What we do

Four parts of one simple business

The company's work covers the whole life of the property it owns, from buying it to letting it, looking after it and, occasionally, selling it.

Property acquisition

The company buys residential or commercial property to hold in its own portfolio. Each purchase is made for the company's own account, to be kept and let, not on behalf of a client or investor.

Lettings of our own property

The company lets the property it owns directly to its tenants, so tenants deal with their landlord rather than an intermediary. It does not let or manage property belonging to anyone else.

Property care

For the length of each tenancy, the company looks after the upkeep and running of the property it owns or leases, arranging repairs and maintenance as the owner.

Property sales

From time to time the company may sell a property from its own portfolio. It sells only property it owns and does not market or sell property for other sellers.

Key facts

Direct, owner-run and plainly set out

These are the facts about the company as they stand on the public register at Companies House.

  • A direct relationship. Tenants deal with the company that owns their property.
  • Own property only. The company does not act for other landlords, buyers or sellers.
  • Owner-managed. The director who runs the company is also its majority shareholder.
